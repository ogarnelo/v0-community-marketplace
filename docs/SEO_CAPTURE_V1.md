# SEO capture v1

## Scope

Adds lightweight acquisition pages without creating deep vertical marketplace pages yet.

## Pages

- `/vende-tus-libros`
  - Targets families with books from the previous school year.
  - Reinforces ISBN, editorial, subject and course.
  - Sends users to publish or search.

- `/como-funciona`
  - Explains Wetudy in simple buyer/seller steps.
  - Keeps the MVP promise: Wetudy facilitates contact and history; delivery and payment are agreed directly between the parties.

## Out of scope

- No programmatic city/school/ISBN pages.
- No uniform-specific vertical page.
- No checkout, shipping, Stripe, Correos or exchange.
